A platform managing the full care system — booking, appointments, prescriptions, shift and staff management, fee management — up to government-level coordination.

Four panels serve four very different user types: Patient (mobile-leaning), Physician (desktop web), Medical Centre, and Backoffice.

// problem

Undefined problems, an outsourced team, and no room for logical gaps.

Most flows started as unknown, undefined problems. Development was outsourced, so any logical inconsistency in the design would surface as a broken product. And the system had to feel uniform across four very different user types.

// approach

Turn ambiguity into flows an outsourced team can't misread.

01
Designed most flows from scratch

Simplified user setup, appointment management, prescription writing and reading, and meetings / consultations.

02
Made every flow logically airtight

The core contribution: complete, uniform flows across all panels, designed defensively so an outsourced dev team could build them without gaps.

03
Architected the design system

A DS Core foundation with 360+ semantic color tokens in full light/dark modes, per-panel component libraries, dedicated handoff files per panel, and a custom Health Icon set.

04
Added AI features for healthcare pain points

Targeted at real healthcare pain points and informed by customer needs, market research, and R&D.

// key work

Flows that cross panels, and the workspace they land in.

→ consultation reason

Physicians write their own pre-consultation questionnaire: steps of typed questions, from yes/no to file upload, or a template to start from. Patients answer it while they book, one step at a time, and can skip it. The answers are waiting on the appointment before it starts, beside the patient's history and their GP's referral.

→ shifts & fees

A physician can work at several medical centres, so hours and fees are set per centre: one weekly grid with a column for each, and a fee for every centre and consultation type. The centre manages the same schedule from its side — each doctor's working hours, and an inbox of fee, shift and exception requests, approved or rejected, with who raised and who reviewed each one on record.

→ the physician's day

Calendar, chats and the day's dashboard, on desktop and mobile, in light and dark. Each one filters by medical centre, because one physician's day can span several, and the three consultation types — in person, video, audio — carry the same icons on every panel.
