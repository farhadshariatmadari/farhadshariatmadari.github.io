Trio

Iran-born, globally-sold enterprise device management — from zero to a Gitex-recognized platform competing with Kandji and Jamf.

trio.so
// context & role

An AI-powered unified endpoint management platform for SMBs and enterprises — device management, endpoint security, identity, and a purpose-built K-12 education suite.

Trio manages devices across macOS, Windows, iOS, and Android; handles endpoint security (EDR, patch and vulnerability management, compliance automation for HIPAA/GDPR/SOC 2) and identity and access (SSO, MFA, zero-trust conditional access). Marketing claims a 15-minute go-live and an 80% cut in IT workload.

// problem

A market of tools IT admins tolerate rather than like.

MDM platforms are notoriously complex — dominated by products that admins put up with rather than enjoy. Trio needed to launch fast and differentiate on simplicity, in a category where the incumbents were Kandji and Jamf.

// approach

Ship the smallest coherent product, then compound on it.

01
Solo for the first ~6 months

Shipped the MVP in 4 months — basic group, user, and device management, centered on macOS MDM enrollment and core actions on enrolled devices.

02
Expanded across three years

Security compliance, integrations, reporting, and the education suite — largely through solo R&D benchmarking against category leaders.

03
Built a custom, scalable design system

A design system spanning the entire platform, so every new surface stayed consistent and fast to build.

04
Grew and led the design team

Added 2 product designers and 1 website designer after MVP; led up to 5 designers at peak. The model: research the feature, solve it with quick ideation, then hand structured tasks to a designer to finalize and hand off.

// key work

Flows, the design system, and the platform surfaces.

Device enrollment — platform and method selection, the core of the MVP surface.
Device detail — status, security automation, and location at a glance.
Policy groups and recommended templates — configuration at scale.
