// key work

The merchant panel: tables, records, and setup.

→ filters & views

Most of the panel is tables: orders, products, inventory, customers. Filtering was designed on a generic table, state by state. Filters sit inside the search field as tags, each one a field, one or more of its options, and is or is not. A filtered table can be saved as a named view. On tablet and phone nothing can be hovered, so there a tag's remove icon is always visible.

→ records

Every record opens in the same frame: its title and actions on top, a breadcrumb back, a card for each concern, and Back and Save at the foot. A collection keeps its products, publishing and theme template on one page. A product with 32 variants gets a two-pane editor: the variants on the left, narrowed by each option, the selected one on the right. Optional fields wait folded as chips, from compare-at price to HS code, so a form starts short.

→ sign-up & settings

A new store starts with an email and a 6-digit code, or a Google or Apple account, and the trial's terms are on screen before anything is asked. Settings open as a sheet over the panel rather than a page of their own, with their own search and navigation, from plan and billing to domains and notifications.
