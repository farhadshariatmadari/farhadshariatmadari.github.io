Behinto

Designing Iran's first AI healthcare assistant — a national-scale medical companion grounded in officially vetted documents doctors can stand behind.

In pilot · launching 2026
[ behinto · consumer app ] Persian-first · RTL · light & dark themes
// context & role

A national, reliable AI healthcare application whose answers are grounded in vetted, current medical documents — built to a standard practicing doctors and the Health Ministry can accept.

Clinician-facing workflows cover patient records, appointments, medications, and charting; the consumer-facing side is in progress. The customer is a team of prominent Iranian doctors — the product and the holding company share the name Behinto.

Client
Behinto — team of prominent Iranian doctors
Role
Lead Product & Experience Designer (contract)
Period
~Feb 2026 – present
// problem

Generic AI chatbots are unacceptable for medical use.

Behinto's challenge is trust: an AI product whose answers doctors will stand behind — in Persian, for a national audience. That means an interaction model where the source of every answer is legible, and where the interface itself signals reliability rather than the improvisation of a general-purpose chatbot.

// approach

Design the trust before designing the chat.

01
Lead all product & experience design

From clinical workflows — patient records, appointments, medications, charting — through to the AI interaction model itself.

02
Build a Persian-first, RTL-first design system

From scratch: a two-tier token architecture with 300+ variables, first-class light and dark themes, and WCAG-AA-calibrated color scales.

03
Make destructive actions feel heavy

Deliberately weighty destructive patterns, so clinicians feel the gravity of actions like discharging a patient before they commit to them.

// key work

The AI interaction model, clinical workflows, and the RTL system.

→ AI visit assistant

The consultation is recorded with explicit consent, transcribed and diarized on review, then distilled into a structured summary — chief complaint, medications, follow-up — the patient can keep or share. The AI never stands in for the doctor, and every screen says so.

→ the full patient app

The everyday home surface and the patient profile — vitals, medications, care team, records, and the daily check-in — built Persian-first and RTL-first across first-class light and dark themes on a WCAG-AA-calibrated token system.
