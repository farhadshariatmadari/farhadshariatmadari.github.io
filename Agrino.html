AI-first Super-app Agriculture

Agrino

Designing Iran's first agriculture application — an AI-powered super-app for farmers, backed by Keshavarzi Bank of Iran.

Foundations shipped · launch targeted ≈ Oct 2026
[ agrino · chat home hero ]
// context & role

A chat-centric super-app for farmers: market information, weather alerts, access to agriculture experts, and commerce — with AI running through all of it.

Consultation chatbots for crop and livestock farmers, AI-powered search for buying and selling products, and custom AI assistants that large agribusinesses can configure for themselves — all answering from documents approved by Keshavarzi Bank.

Client
Keshavarzi Bank of Iran (Agricultural Bank)
Role
Lead Product & Experience Designer (contract)
Period
~Oct 2025 – present
// problem

Powerful AI, for a mixed-skill rural audience.

Agrino has to make AI consultation, commerce, and expert access usable by farmers across a wide range of digital literacy — and every answer has to trace back to sources Keshavarzi Bank has approved. The design challenge is making something genuinely powerful feel plain-spoken and trustworthy.

// approach

Design straight from the bank's problems.

01
End-to-end design from stakeholder sessions

Ran the work directly off sessions with the bank, translating their problems into customized, optimized flows.

02
Built the foundations

A full token system — 300+ variables, light + dark from day one — plus plain-language content rules written for mixed-skill rural users.

03
Components & flows, in progress

Building out the component library and the core flows on top of the shipped foundations.
