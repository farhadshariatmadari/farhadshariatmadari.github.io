// problem

A first-of-its-kind service, held back by friction.

As Iran's first e-bike and scooter sharing service, Zeero was defining the category as it went. Reservation and ride flows carried friction and operational errors, and new riders were dropping out before completing a first ride.

// approach

Research the friction, then redesign around it.

01
Complete mobile-app UX redesign

Spearheaded a redesign of the whole app rather than iterating screen by screen.

02
Established a scalable design system

Built the foundation the redesigned product would grow on.

03
Optimized reservation & ride flows

Worked with engineers and PMs to cut operational errors out of the core flows.

04
Ran interviews & surveys

Found pain points and refined features directly against what riders were telling us.

// key work

The reservation-to-ride flow, and the money it moves.

Core flow

Reserve, ride, return

The path a rider walks every time — rebuilt to strip out the operational errors.

Find & reserve. Filterable map of nearby bikes and scooters with live battery, price, and a one-tap reservation card.
Unlock & ride. Reservation countdown, slide-to-unlock, and in-ride controls with support one tap away.
Verify & end. A required photo of the parked, locked vehicle closes the trip — cutting disputes and misuse.
Account

Wallet, packages & profile

Making balance, debts, and ride packages legible so riders always know where they stand.

Wallet. Balance, top-up, and a filterable transaction log with plain-language reasons for every charge and penalty.
Packages. Ride bundles compared at a glance — vehicle type, duration, and validity, with promos surfaced clearly.
Profile. Account hub tying wallet, packages, ride history, and outstanding debt into one clear overview.
