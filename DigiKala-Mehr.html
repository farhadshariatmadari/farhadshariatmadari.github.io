DigiKala Mehr

As sole designer at Iran's largest e-commerce company, I redesigned its charity platform and grew purchase conversion from 0.4% to 2.8% — double the sitewide average.

mehr.digikala.com
// context & role

DigiKala Mehr lets people support charities by buying the products those charities actually need — directly on Digikala.

Charities onboard through their own panels and create "concepts" — campaigns that users can contribute to. I led the design end to end as the only designer, working directly with management.

Company
Digikala
Role
Senior Product Designer — freelance, sole designer
Period
May 2023 – May 2024
// problem

Goodwill traffic that wasn't converting into purchases.

Mehr's purchase conversion sat at 0.4% — far below Digikala's 1.4% sitewide average. People arrived willing to help, but the path from intention to a completed charitable purchase was leaking them.

0.4%
Mehr conversion, before
1.4%
Digikala sitewide average
// approach

Redesign the whole path, from charity onboarding to checkout.

01
Charity onboarding panels

Rebuilt how charities join and manage themselves on the platform, so supply-side setup wasn't a bottleneck.

02
The concept / campaign model

Reworked how charities create "concepts" and how users understand and contribute to them.

03
A user-centered contribution & purchase flow

Redesigned the core experience of choosing what to give and completing the purchase — the part where conversion was being lost.

PURCHASE CONVERSION
