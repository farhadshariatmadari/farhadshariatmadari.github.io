Safes

Took a parental-control app from 500 to 500,000 downloads in four months — by making proper protection something parents get without configuring everything by hand.

[ safes · parent & kid apps hero ]
// context & role

A parental control and school-safety app spanning screen time, content filtering, app rules, location, and classroom management.

Safes runs across Android, iOS, and Chromebook, with separate parent and kid apps. I enhanced the MVP end to end, established the design system, and simplified the experience for both parents and children.

Company
SternX Technology GmbH
Role
Senior Product Designer
Platforms
Android · iOS · Chromebook
// problem

Protection that demanded too much setup.

The MVP existed, but setup asked parents for heavy manual configuration — a major drop-off point. Parents wanting to keep their kids safe were stalling before they ever got protection in place.

// approach

Replace configuration with ready-to-use roles.

Signature feature
Ready-to-use profile roles

Pre-built, automated rule sets tailored by a child's age, gender, and region — so parents get proper protection immediately, without having to configure every rule themselves.
